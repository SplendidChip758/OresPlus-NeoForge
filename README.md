# OresPlus

OresPlus is a NeoForge mod that expands the vanilla progression with new ores, machines and world generation. The project targets Minecraft 1.21.4.

## Features

- **Ores and Materials**
  - Bauxite, Salt, Hematite and Magnetite ores generate underground and drop new raw items.
  - Processed materials such as Aluminum, Pig Iron and Steel ingots provide new crafting options.
  - Limestone blocks and Refractory Bricks for advanced building recipes.

- **Machines**
  - The **Crusher** grinds raw ores into dusts for better resource yields.
  - The **Simple Kiln** bakes materials and serves as a workstation for the Smelter villager.
  - The **Simple Smelter** offers an early smelting upgrade over the vanilla furnace.
  - The **Smelter** multiblock consists of Controller and IO blocks and can be enhanced with Smelter Upgrade Modules.

- **Tools, Armor and Items**
  - Complete tool sets and armor crafted from Pig Iron and Steel.
  - Wrenches for interaction with machines and Upgrade Modules to improve the Smelter.

- **World Generation**
  - Custom ore placement rules for all new ores.
  - A **Branching Coal Vein** feature that creates sprawling coal deposits for large mining operations.

- **Villagers**
  - A new Smelter profession uses the kiln as its point of interest.
